**Student Eco Wallet (ESSE)**
About the Project

Student Eco Wallet (ESSE) is a student-focused digital platform designed to improve campus resource and environmental management.

The website allows students to report problems around campus, such as leaking taps, overflowing bins, damaged facilities, and other environmental or maintenance issues.

The platform encourages students to actively participate in maintaining a cleaner, more sustainable, and better-managed campus.

**How It Works**
Report an Issue – Students identify and report environmental or infrastructure-related problems.
Submit Details – The issue and relevant information are submitted through the platform.
Location-Based Reporting – Reports can be associated with their location on campus.
Verification – Administrators can review and verify submitted reports.
Eco Points – Students can earn Eco Points for verified contributions.
Rewards – Eco Points can be used for campus-related rewards and incentives.
**Key Features**
📝 Student issue reporting
📍 Location-based issue identification
📊 Student and administrator dashboards
✅ Issue verification
🌱 Eco Points system
🎁 Reward-based participation
🌓 Light and dark mode
💻 User-friendly interface
**Purpose**
The main purpose of ESSE is to create a more interactive approach to campus resource management by involving students in identifying and reporting problems.

Instead of relying only on campus staff to discover maintenance and environmental issues, ESSE provides students with a direct way to report problems and contribute to a cleaner and more sustainable campus.
